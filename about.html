The ministry

A shared altar. A shared fire.

The story of two lives woven together in covenant and calling, set apart for the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Pastor Olumide Oni & Pastor (Mrs.) Oluwatoyin Oni

The calling

Four mantles. One anointing.

Prophet Evangelist Pastor Teacher

Pastor Olumide Oni moves in the fullness of the fourfold ministry: a prophet who hears and declares, an evangelist whose message wins souls, a pastor who shepherds with compassion, and a teacher whose word dispels doubt and builds faith. Above all, he is a husband and father, a man who orders his private life before God first, and whose public ministry flows from that altar.

Together in ministry

Two standing together before the Lord

Pastor Olumide Oni and his wife, Pastor (Mrs.) Oluwatoyin Oni, have laboured side by side in the vineyard of the Lord for many years, blessed with children, steadfast in covenant and united in purpose.

Wherever the Master has sent them, they have gone. Through every reassignment, every new field of ministry and every season of trial, they have stood together as co-labourers: a living testimony that a house built on prayer, faith and the unchanging Word of God cannot be shaken.

Pastoral journey

A path of obedience and appointment

From region to region within the Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries, every assignment a new altar and every relocation a fresh commissioning.

The beginningMFM Kubwa

The starting point of a long obedience, where the call was first formally set in motion.

First reassignmentMFM Lugbe Region, Abuja

Transferred to serve in the Lugbe Region of the Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.

Capital serviceMFM Utako, then Wuye, Abuja

Reassigned to MFM Utako, which later became MFM Wuye, the International Headquarters Annex in Abuja.

Present assignmentMFM North America HQ, Prayer City

Transferred to the MFM North America Headquarters, known as Prayer City, where he serves as Principal Regional Overseer.

Current mandate

Principal Regional Overseer, Mega Region II, North America

Ministry of the Word

A voice that dispels doubt and demonstrates power

Pastor Olumide Oni is a dynamic minister of God's Word. His teachings carry a remarkable ability to silence doubt while unveiling the extraordinary power of God, through prayers that produce tangible results, astonishing manifestations and awe-inspiring miracles.

His personal encounters through life's trials stand as a living testament to the strength of God to deliver His people in every season. He ministers not from theory, but from the altar where he himself has stood and seen the hand of the Lord.
